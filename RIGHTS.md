# Rights and naming

The current eBible.org World English Bible rights page states that the World
English Bible is public domain and may be copied, published, distributed,
quoted, and shared. It also identifies “World English Bible” as an eBible.org
trademark and asks that altered text not be called the World English Bible.
See the [eBible.org WEB rights page](https://ebible.org/engwebp/copyright.htm)
for the authoritative current wording.

This project is an independent software distribution, not an official
eBible.org or World English Bible distribution. Preserve the bundled notices
and do not label modified Scripture text “World English Bible.” The current
rights review is distinct from historical rights evidence recorded for older
source lineages. This summary is informational and is not legal advice.

No Ollama service or model weights are bundled. Other third-party notices
applicable to the software are included in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
