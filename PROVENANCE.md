# Provenance and lineage

The bundled Scripture payload follows a new ENGWEBP v2 source lineage based
on source archives acquired from the documented eBible.org Scripture
downloads. The public artifact binds that lineage to its source-baseline and
candidate identities in `public_artifact_manifest_v2.json`.

The exact historical authoritative USFM bytes for the earlier lineage are
unavailable. Historical USFM equivalence is therefore **NOT COMPARABLE**;
this release makes no claim that the new source lineage is equivalent to
earlier local production data. The historical production-tree fingerprint
whose derivation remains unresolved is not redefined or replaced here.

The accepted source archives have an independently verified retained copy on
separate physical media. This is physical-media redundancy, not geographic
redundancy. The owner-mediated copy time was not separately recorded. A later
additive retention verification satisfied the previously pending retention
requirement; internal acceptance, approval, and retention records are not
part of this public metadata or the payload.

No historical artifact approval is inherited by this new lineage.
